About the studio

Built on drawings, not templates

NIVORA DESIGNZ was founded in February 2026 as a design-led studio covering architecture, interiors, 3D visualization, and turnkey execution. We work across two markets: interior and architectural projects in Mumbai, and full bungalow developments across Gujarat.

Every project starts as a drawing and a conversation — how a family actually moves through a home, how a business actually uses an office — before it becomes a floor plan, a render, or a finished room.

Our first project, a commercial office interior, was completed in April 2026. We are currently executing a G+1 bungalow in Kutch, Gujarat, managed end-to-end with regular site visits and structured remote oversight.
